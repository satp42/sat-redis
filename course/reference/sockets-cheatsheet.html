Sockets cheat sheet

The socket calls sat-redis uses, what each one means in the phone-call picture, and the traps the docs warn about.

Server side, in order

CallPhone pictureNotes
socket.socket(AF_INET, SOCK_STREAM)Get a handsetIPv4 + TCP
s.setsockopt(SOL_SOCKET, SO_REUSEADDR, 1)Allow reusing a door that was just closedBefore bind. Skips the TIME_WAIT "Address already in use" wait.
s.bind(("127.0.0.1", 6380))Claim a doorPort 0 = let the OS pick a free one (tests do this)
s.listen()Become the receptionistWaiting callers queue up
conn, addr = s.accept()Pick up the next callBlocks. Returns a new socket for that caller.
conn.recv(1024)ListenUp to 1024 bytes, maybe fewer. b"" = hung up.
conn.sendall(data)Speak all of itLoops until every byte is sent. Plain send may stop early.
conn.close() or with conn:Hang upwith closes even if an error is raised
s.getsockname()Read your own door numberReturns (host, port)

Client side

CallNotes
socket.create_connection(("127.0.0.1", 6380), timeout=2)Dial and connect in one step
nc localhost 6380Type lines by hand, see the raw reply
printf 'PING\r\n' | nc -w1 localhost 6380 | od -cSee every byte, including \r and \n
lsof -i :6380Who is holding this port? (find a server you forgot to stop)

Errors you will meet

ErrorMeaningWhat to do
OSError: [Errno 48] Address already in useSomething already holds that port, or SO_REUSEADDR was not setlsof -i :6380, stop it, check the flag
ConnectionRefusedErrorNobody is listening at that doorStart the server first
ConnectionResetErrorThe caller slammed the phone (crashed or reset)End that one conversation, keep serving
BrokenPipeErrorYou spoke after the caller already leftSame: end that conversation

Both of the last two are subclasses of ConnectionError.

The three truths

1. TCP is a hose, not envelopes: order is kept, message boundaries are not. 2. recv(n) and send may handle fewer bytes than you asked. 3. recv returning b"" means the other side closed, forever. [Python Socket HOWTO]
